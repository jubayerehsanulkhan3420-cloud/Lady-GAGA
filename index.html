More Than A Superstar.

Lady Gaga, born Stefani Joanne Angelina Germanotta on March 28, 1986, is an American singer, songwriter and actress.

Born in Manhattan, New York City, she developed a passion for music at a young age. She began playing piano as a child and later pursued professional music studies.

Her unique voice, creative performances, experimental fashion and powerful songwriting have made her an internationally recognized artist.

BEYOND THE MUSIC

Her Achievements

Music

Lady Gaga has received numerous Grammy Awards and international music honors for her songwriting and performances.

Acting

She starred in A Star Is Born, House of Gucci and other major screen productions. Her performance in A Star Is Born earned her an Academy Award for Shallow.

Philanthropy

She co-founded the Born This Way Foundation to support young people's mental wellness and build kinder communities.
